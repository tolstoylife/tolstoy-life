# The Great Sin

**Великий грех · Leo Tolstoy · 1905 · PSS Tom 36, pp. 206–230**

Tolstoy's 1905 essay on the private ownership of land — the work in which he
calls land monopoly «великий грех», the great sin at the root of the people's
poverty, and endorses Henry George's single tax as its remedy. Written during
the 1905 Revolution.

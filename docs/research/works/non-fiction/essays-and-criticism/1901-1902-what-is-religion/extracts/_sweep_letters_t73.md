## Five most quotable letters — verbatim Russian + working English + significance

### 1. To Н. Н. Ге (сын), 26 Aug 1901 — v73_146_H_N_Gesynu

**Verbatim:**
> То, что при вас писал, кончил и теперь пишу о религии, об ее отсутствии и въехал в работу.

**Working English:** "What I was writing when you were here I have finished, and now I am writing about religion, about its absence, and I have gotten into the work."

**Significance:** Earliest extant statement that the religion article has begun; the phrase «въехал в работу» (literally "driven into the work") signals full engagement immediately after completing a prior piece. Date of letter is confirmed 26 Aug 1901.

---

### 2. To В. В. Стасов, 28 Aug 1901 — v73_151_V_V_Stasovu

**Verbatim:**
> Я хвораю, но не перестаю работать, à tort или à raison думая, что моя работа нужна.

**Working English:** "I am unwell, but I do not stop working, rightly or wrongly thinking that my work is needed."

**Significance:** Written just two days after the Ge letter; shows Tolstoy pressing on despite illness at Yasnaya Polyana before the move to Gaspra. The French aside («à tort ou à raison») catches a flash of self-irony: he half-doubts the necessity yet cannot stop.

---

### 3. To Г. А. Русанов, 5 Sept 1901 — v73_154_G_A_Rusanovu

**Verbatim:**
> Теперь я занят статьей о религии: определение ее и выяснение ее отсутствия в нашем мире и ужасных последствиях этого. Очень меня занимает эта работа.

**Working English:** "I am now occupied with an article on religion: defining it and elucidating its absence in our world and the terrible consequences of this. This work absorbs me greatly."

**Significance:** The clearest early programme-statement: definition of religion + diagnosis of its absence + consequences. This three-part structure maps directly onto the treatise's architecture. Last letter before the Gaspra move (5 Sept).

---

### 4. To П. И. Бирюков, 31 Dec 1901 — v73_197_P_I_Biryukovu

**Verbatim:**
> О религии кончил и на днях отправлю.

**Working English:** "On religion I have finished and will send it off in the coming days."

**Significance:** Completion announcement. Written from Gaspra on New Year's Eve 1901, establishing the end of active composition. The phrase «на днях отправлю» confirms the manuscript had not yet gone to Chertkov at that moment.

---

### 5. To Ф. Х. Граубергер, 20 Jan 1902 — v73_209_F_X_Graubergeru

**Verbatim:**
> Я совершенно согласен с тем, что вы пишете. Я понемногу приходил к этому убеждению и теперь пришел окончательно, что и выразил в своей статье «Что такое религия и в чем ее сущность?», которая теперь, вероятно, печатается у Черткова.

**Working English:** "I entirely agree with what you write. I was gradually coming to this conviction and have now arrived at it definitively, which I expressed in my article 'What Is Religion and What Is Its Essence?', which is now, probably, being printed at Chertkov's."

**Significance:** Only letter in this group that names the article by its full Russian title and places it in the press at Chertkov's Free Age Press operation. Written 20 Jan 1902, ~3 weeks after the Biryukov "finished" letter — the manuscript had reached Chertkov in the interim.

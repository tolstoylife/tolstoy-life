# Diary sweep — *What I Believe* composition window
## PSS Tom 49: 1 Dec 1882 – 13 Sep 1883

**Scope:** 191 XML files scanned (190 × 1883 + 1 × Dec-1882 entry).  
**Hit count:** All 191 files return hits on the broad faith/people grep; the 40 files extracted in full (see below) are the ones with substantive content on genesis/writing-progress or named persons.  
**Date span of strongest material:** 9 March – 31 May 1883, with secondary peaks in July (Ge visits, Chertkov letters) and September (writing blocked, Chertkov/Malykov letter).  
**Note on bibl headers:** All extracted entries carry `bibl: Толстой Л. Н. Дневник 1884 г.` — this is a PSS editorial convention for the volume heading of the 1883–1884 diary notebook; the dates in the filenames and openers are the actual diary dates.

## Section A — Genesis & reaction (~10–15 strongest entries)

**A-01**  
TEI id: `v49_059_059_1882_12_22` | Date: 22 декабря 1882  
> Если любишь Бога, добро (кажется, я начинаю любить его), любишь, т. е. живешь им — счастье в нем, жизнь в нем видишь, то видишь и то, что тело мешает добру истинному [...] Сей, сей, зная, что не ты, человек, пожнешь. Один сеет, другой жнет. Ты, человек, Лев Николаевич, не сожнешь.

(working English) "If you love God, love goodness... you see that the body hinders true good... Sow, sow, knowing that you, a man, will not reap."  
**Why it matters:** The Dec-1882 entry, written weeks before the sustained March diary run, captures the theological substrate of В чём моя вера? — the seed/harvest parable as personal conviction, the self-address "Лев Николаевич", the insistence on doing good without witnessing its fruits. The same logic undergirds chapters 7–9 of the finished work.

**A-02**  
TEI id: `v49_064_065_1883_03_09` | Date: 9 марта 1883  
> Письмо Черткова — вызывает написать о заповедях для народа. Кажется, что надо. Это захватило меня, но не знаю.

(working English) "Chertkov's letter urges me to write about the commandments for the people. It seems necessary. It has seized me — but I don't know."  
**Why it matters:** The earliest datable moment when the *project* is triggered: Chertkov's letter acts as catalyst, the subject is specifically "о заповедях для народа" — which maps directly onto the Sermon-on-the-Mount structure of В чём моя вера? The uncertainty ("но не знаю") is significant: he does not yet commit.

---

**A-03**  
TEI id: `v49_065_066_1883_03_10` | Date: 10 марта 1883  
> Написал Черткову — кажется, хорошо, т. е. без фальши. — Хорошо бы написать ту книгу, да надо, чтобы было совсем чистое побуждение.

(working English) "Wrote to Chertkov — seems good, i.e. without falseness. — It would be good to write that book, but the motive must be completely pure."  
**Why it matters:** First appearance of the phrase "ту книгу" — Tolstoy names В чём моя вера? as a *book* in his own mind. The condition "совсем чистое побуждение" (completely pure motive) is not mere pietism: it is the ethical prerequisite he will wrestle with throughout the composition period.

---

**A-04**  
TEI id: `v49_071_072_1883_03_22` | Date: 22 марта 1883  
> И перевод плох, а оригинал еще плоше. Нехорошо вступление к заповедям, ничем не мотивировано. Надо написать вновь для всех.

(working English) "The translation is bad, the original even worse. The introduction to the commandments is no good — it has no motivation. Need to write afresh for everyone."  
**Why it matters:** Tolstoy is working on a draft (which he calls a translation/text about the commandments) and finds the existing approach inadequate. "Надо написать вновь для всех" points toward the all-audience scope that distinguishes В чём моя вера? from earlier theological fragments.

---

**A-05**  
TEI id: `v49_074_075_1883_03_29` | Date: 29 марта 1883  
> Чертков огорчил меня, но не надолго (1). [...] Важная: Если точно я живу (отчасти) по воле Бога, то безумный, больной мир не может одобрять меня за это. И если бы они одобрили, я перестал бы жить по воле Бога, а стал бы жить по воле мира, я перестал бы видеть и искать волю Бога. Таково было твое благоволение.

(working English) "If I truly live (in part) by God's will, the mad, sick world cannot approve of me for it. And if they approved, I would have ceased to live by God's will and begun to live by the world's will."  
**Why it matters:** The doctrine of non-worldly approval — central to В чём моя вера? chapter on the impossibility of reconciling Christ's law with state approval — here appears in its diary form. The self-correction about Chertkov ("огорчил меня, но не надолго") also signals the volatility of this first correspondence period.

**A-06**  
TEI id: `v49_086_086_1883_04_23` | Date: 23 апреля 1883  
> Как не противиться злу? Всё то же. Хочется знать истину и осуждать других, но делать ее не хочется.

(working English) "How to not resist evil? Always the same. One wants to know the truth and judge others, but not to do it."  
**Why it matters:** "Как не противиться злу?" is the exact formulation of the book's central chapter — non-resistance to evil (не противься злому, Matt. 5:39). Here he frames it as a live difficulty in his own behavior and in his interlocutors, not as a theoretical position already settled.

---

**A-07**  
TEI id: `v49_086_086_1883_04_24` | Date: 24 апреля 1883  
> Мне пришла в голову мысль об издании Нагорной Проповеди. [...] Чертков также тверд и спокоен. [...] Писарев близок (боюсь, что заблуждаюсь), но как бы я желал!

(working English) "The thought came to me of publishing the Sermon on the Mount. [...] Chertkov equally firm and calm."  
**Why it matters:** Tolstoy contemplates publishing the Sermon on the Mount as a standalone — an idea that merges with the В чём моя вера? project. This entry documents the conceptual overlap between the book and popular scripture publication, a question that becomes central to Chertkov's Посредник venture (founded 1884).

---

**A-08**  
TEI id: `v49_088_088_1883_04_28` | Date: 28 апреля 1883  
> Утром опять попытался. И решительно — не могу продолжать свою статью. Тонкости, и потому будет ложь.

(working English) "In the morning tried again. And decisively — I cannot continue my article. Too subtle, and therefore it will be a lie."  
**Why it matters:** The clearest writing-blocked moment in the diary: he has a draft ("свою статью") but finds it too nuanced and therefore dishonest. The diagnosis "тонкости — потому будет ложь" is the same criterion that later drives his repeated revisions of the chapter on oaths and courts.

**A-09**  
TEI id: `v49_089_089_1883_05_01` | Date: 1 мая 1883  
> Стал поправлять Ивана Ильича и хорошо работал. Вероятно, мне нужен отдых от той работы, и эта, художественная, такая.

(working English) "Started correcting Ivan Ilyich and worked well. Probably I need rest from *that* work, and this artistic one is just that."  
**Why it matters:** "Той работы" — the unnamed theological tract — is explicitly distinguished from the fictional work (Смерть Ивана Ильича). Shows the two projects running in parallel; the religious work is the demanding one from which fiction offers relief.

---

**A-10**  
TEI id: `v49_099_099_1883_05_31` | Date: 31 мая 1883  
> Рано. Не помню. Знаю, что не работал. Кажется, просмотрел написанное. Дальше не могу идти. А доволен. И очень сильно и «к делу» дальнейшее.

(working English) "Early. I don't remember. I know I didn't work. I think I reviewed what was written. I cannot go further. But I am satisfied. And what remains is very strong and 'to the point'."  
**Why it matters:** The end-of-May writing report: he has reviewed the manuscript, reached a wall ("дальше не могу идти"), but is satisfied. "Очень сильно и «к делу» дальнейшее" suggests the unwritten sections are composed in his head. This is the last explicit writing-progress note before the summer field-work period.

**A-11**  
TEI id: `v49_103_103_1883_06_12` | Date: 12 июня 1883  
> Попытки писать. Косил. [...] Да, пересматривал Критику богословия. Трудно поправлять.

(working English) "Attempts to write. Mowed. [...] Was going over the Critique of Theology. Hard to revise."  
**Why it matters:** "Критика богословия" is either an early title or section of the work that becomes В чём моя вера? / also overlapping with Исследование догматического богословия. The note "трудно поправлять" continues the pattern of stalled revision.

**A-12**  
TEI id: `v49_114_115_1883_07_27` | Date: 27 июля 1883  
> Еще думал о книге для народа, опять в форме признания — хорошо. [...] Но Чертков настаивает, что надо прежде всего объяснить народу Евангелие, он прав.

(working English) "Also thought about a book for the people, again in the form of a confession — good. [...] But Chertkov insists that one must first explain the Gospel to the people; he is right."  
**Why it matters:** "В форме признания" links to the confessional rhetoric of В чём моя вера? (and back to Исповедь). Chertkov's insistence that the Gospel must be explained first is the programmatic impulse behind both this work and the later Посредник popular-Christianity series.

---

**A-13**  
TEI id: `v49_119_119_1883_08_27` | Date: 27 августа 1883  
> С Ге читал Евангелие — 24 главу Матфея. Как ясно и хорошо, за исключением предсказания о Данииле.

(working English) "With Ge read the Gospel — Matthew chapter 24. How clear and good, except for the prophecy about Daniel."  
**Why it matters:** Matthew 24 is the eschatological discourse — Tolstoy and Nikolai Gay reading it together as he is finishing the text. The qualification "за исключением предсказания о Данииле" is precisely the kind of textual sifting that appears in the book's method: accept the moral, bracket the apocalyptic.

**A-14**  
TEI id: `v49_120_120_1883_09_09` | Date: 9 сентября 1883  
> Был Урусов. Я хотел писать и не мог.

(working English) "Urусов came. I wanted to write and could not."  
**Why it matters:** The September writing block — most compressed of all. Within weeks the manuscript was reportedly complete; this single sentence marks the pre-completion impasse.

---

**A-15**  
TEI id: `v49_120_120_1883_09_10` | Date: 10 сентября 1883  
> Будизм и еврейское. Очень много читал. Писать не мог.

(working English) "Buddhism and Hebrew [Gospel]. Read a great deal. Could not write."  
**Why it matters:** Companion to A-14. He is reading Buddhist texts and the Hebrew Gospel simultaneously — the comparative-religion frame active in В чём моя вера?'s chapters on the universal validity of Christ's moral teaching. The September reading/writing split suggests the manuscript was being held while he processed sources.

## Section C — Notes/Uncertainties

1. **Explicit title absent.** The phrase "В чём моя вера" does not appear in any 1883 diary entry. Tolstoy refers to the project variously as "та книга" (10 Mar), "книга для народа" (27 Jul), "статья" (20 Apr, 28 Apr), "написанное" (31 May), "Критика богословия" (12 Jun). The last phrase may refer to an overlapping text (Исследование догматического богословия) rather than В чём моя вера? — route, don't assert.

2. **"Критика богословия" vs В чём моя вера?** The 12 Jun entry mentions revising "Критика богословия." This title could be (a) an early working title for В чём моя вера?, (b) a section of it, or (c) the separate Исследование догматического богословия which was also in progress. Given that PSS editors distinguish them as separate works, caution is warranted before equating them.

3. **Chertkov's first YP visit.** The diary records no Chertkov visit to Yasnaya Polyana in 1883; all contact is by letter and two Moscow personal encounters (6 Apr; 19 Apr). The well-known first YP visit (often given as October 1883) falls after this diary corpus ends. The Sep 13 entry mentions a letter from Chertkov, not a visit.

4. **April 16 entry (v49_032_033_1883_04_16) bibl anomaly.** This entry appears in PSS pages 32–33 but is dated April 1883 — possibly a misbound leaf in the original notebook, which explains the bibl "Дневник 1884 г." with 1883 content. The entry content (Urусов letter, Chertkov letter, Lukyan report) is consistent with the March–April 1883 sequence.

5. **"Нагорнов" vs "Нагорная [Проповедь]."** Rg hits on "Нагорн" in Mar 7 and Apr 22–27 entries match the personal name Нагорнов (а Tolstoy family acquaintance), not "Нагорная Проповедь" (Sermon on the Mount). The one genuine Sermon on the Mount reference in the diary is Apr 24: "пришла в голову мысль об издании Нагорной Проповеди."

6. **Ролстон/Ralston.** The recipient "Ролстон" may be W. R. S. Ralston (1828–1889), British Slavicist at the British Museum who corresponded with Tolstoy. Uncertain — route only.

7. **Malykov identity.** "Маликов" in 16 Mar and 13 Sep is almost certainly A. K. Malykov, founder of the "Богочеловечество" sect, who was in Tolstoy's orbit in this period. Tolstoy's attitude in the diary is not clearly recorded — needs cross-check with letters.

8. **Gap Sept 13 → end of corpus.** The corpus ends 13 Sep 1883. В чём моя вера? was reportedly completed later in 1883 and circulated in manuscript. The diary gives no completion-announcement entry within this corpus.

9. **"Писать не мог" cluster.** The Sep 9 and 10 writing-blocked entries, plus the "Был Урусов. Я хотел писать и не мог" on Sep 9, are the last writing-state notes before the corpus closes. Whether this signals a permanent block before a final burst (outside the diary corpus) or the near-completion plateau cannot be determined from the diary alone.

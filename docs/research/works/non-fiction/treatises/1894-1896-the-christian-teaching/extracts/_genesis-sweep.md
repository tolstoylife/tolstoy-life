# Genesis sweep — *The Christian Teaching* (Христианское учение, March 1894 – Oct 1896)

Composition-years witness sweep of Tolstoy's diaries (PSS Toms 52–53) and letters (Toms 67, 84, 87) for (1) the genesis, strain and self-understanding of the work, and (2) the people around it. All Russian is copied byte-for-byte from the `extract_tei.py --choice=reg --notes=auto` extracts under this folder; superscript digits (¹²³…) are the editors' footnote markers carried through from the TEI. English is a working gloss, not a published translation.

**Naming arc:** Tolstoy calls the work **«катехизис» / «катихизис»** (catechism) through 1894 and into late 1895, then **«изложение веры»** (exposition of [my] faith) from spring 1896 on — the rename tracks his decision to drop the question-and-answer catechism form (see 2 Nov 1894 diary and 2 Nov 1894 letter to Chertkov).

---

## Genesis passages (date order)

### 1894-03-05 — Letter to D. A. Khilkov (`v67_069_D_A_Xilkovu`)
Not about the catechism itself, but the clearest early statement of the *culte / art-and-religion* problem that the book's later chapters wrestle with — and it shows Chertkov already the operational partner on the related Khilkov-children affair.

> Я думаю, что то, чего вы требуете, это общения более тесного, более определенного, более влиятельного, такого, которое могло бы привлекать к себе и поддерживать нерассуждающих, не рассудком, а сердцем живущих людей: детей, женщин, слабых и всех людей в периоды ослабления […] но думаю, что нет ничего опаснее и вреднее, как придумывать его или удерживать отживший старый.

*(working English)* "What you are asking for is a closer, more definite, more influential communion — one that could attract and sustain the non-reasoning people who live by the heart and not the mind: children, women, the weak, and all people in their periods of weakening […] but I think nothing is more dangerous and harmful than to invent such a thing or to keep up an old one that has outlived itself."

**Significance:** the "children, women, the weak" audience that the *Ваничка-и-дворник* test will crystallise six months later is already here; and the suspicion of *придумывать* (contriving) a religious form prefigures the book's anti-clerical core.

---

### 1894-04-21 — Diary (`v52_114_116_1894_04_21`)
First diary mention. He frames the catechism as something he should not have started.

> За всё это время писал предисловие к Мопассану, кажется уяснилось вполне, и еще катехизис, который напрасно затеял, не окончив начатого.

*(working English)* "All this time I was writing the preface to Maupassant — it seems to have come fully clear — and also the catechism, which I undertook in vain, without finishing what I'd already begun."

**Significance:** the work is born already burdened by guilt about competing projects (the Maupassant preface, the art essay) — the strain that recurs for two and a half years.

---

### 1894-05-03 — Diary (`v52_116_117_1894_05_03`)
> Всё думал о катехизисе. Это гораздо — как всегда бывает — серьезнее и важнее и труднее, чем я думал.

*(working English)* "Kept thinking about the catechism. As always, it is far more serious and important and difficult than I'd thought."

**Significance:** the scope dawns on him — the first of many admissions that the work outgrows the modest "catechism" label.

---

### 1894-05-08 (or 9) — Letter to N. N. Ge the son (`v67_122_N_N_Gesynu`)
The single most important self-description of the work's *purpose*, in his own words, naming both names («катехизис — изложение веры») and stating the children-and-simple-people test before the famous Vanichka formulation.

> Как бы хотелось послужить вам в деле воспитания детей составлением катехизиса — изложения веры, который я пишу теперь. Хочется, чтобы отвечало на те вопросы, которые всегда одинаково возникают во всех детях и простых людях, и ответить убедительно, доказательно и бесспорно и без спора; и чтобы было коротко и понятно. Работаю над этим и очень буду рад, если удастся.

*(working English)* "How I should like to serve you in the matter of educating the children by composing the catechism — the exposition of faith — that I am writing now. I want it to answer the questions that always arise, identically, in all children and simple people, and to answer convincingly, demonstrably, indisputably and without dispute; and to be short and clear. I am working on it and shall be very glad if it comes off."

**Significance:** the design brief in one sentence — answer the universal child's/simple-person's questions, convincingly and briefly. The «катехизис — изложение веры» apposition shows both titles already coexisting in his mind in May 1894.

---

### 1894-05-15 — Diary (`v52_117_118_1894_05_15`)
> Ничего не писал. Слаб. Катехизис мало подвинулся, но, кажется, выйдет.

*(working English)* "Wrote nothing. Weak. The catechism has moved little, but it seems it will come off."

**Significance:** the rhythm of the whole genesis — illness, stalled work, cautious hope — set early.

---

### 1894-06-20 — Letter to E. I. Popov (`v67_163_E_I_Popovu`)
> Думаю постоянно, хотя и не пишу теперь, об изложении, главное, о самом учении жизни, о самой сущности его […] всё яснее и яснее мне становится то, что только единое на потребу — одно нужно: блюсти в себе свое божественное я и растить его […]

*(working English)* "I keep thinking constantly — though I'm not writing it now — about the exposition, chiefly about the teaching of life itself, about its very essence […] it becomes ever clearer to me that only one thing is needful: to guard one's divine self within and grow it […]"

**Significance:** he calls it «изложение» here, names «учение жизни» / «учение блага» territory, and states the book's central doctrine (guard and grow the divine self) — to Popov, who will be one of its copyists.

---

### 1894-07-27 — Diary (`v52_130_130_1894_07_27`)
The high-water mark of confidence in 1894 — the whole plan finally clear.

> Вчера писал и нынче вполне уяснил себе в голове весь план Катехизиса. Хорошо, радостно.

*(working English)* "Yesterday I wrote, and today I have made fully clear in my head the whole plan of the Catechism. Good, joyful."

**Significance:** the rare unclouded moment; the architecture is settled (it will later be torn up).

---

### 1894-09-04 — Letter to A. A. Tolstaya (`v67_218_A_A_Tolstoj`)
To his Orthodox kinswoman — he anticipates she will dislike it, and gives the cleanest single-line statement of form and aim.

> Я занят теперь работой, которая, боюсь, неприятна вам, но которую я чувствую себя обязанным делать: это изложение в самой короткой и всем понятной форме своего религиозного исповедания веры.

*(working English)* "I am now occupied with a work that, I fear, is disagreeable to you, but which I feel obliged to do: this is the exposition, in the shortest and universally comprehensible form, of my religious confession of faith."

**Significance:** «в самой короткой и всем понятной форме» — the brevity-and-universal-clarity criterion, stated to the one correspondent who would reject the content. Sense of obligation («чувствую себя обязанным») over desire.

---

### 1894-10-22 — Letter to E. I. Popov (`v67_264_E_I_Popovu`) — KEYSTONE
Contains BOTH marquee phrases verbatim — «опасность отвлеченного умствования» and the «Ваничка и дворник» test.

> Я не могу даже сказать, что продолжаю свою работу, дня три ничего не пишу и чувствую необходимость всё изменить и всё начать сначала. Я увидел на этой работе опасность отвлеченного умствования. Не успеешь оглянуться, как забредешь в страшные дебри. Кажется, что остановился во-время. Поверка одна — доступность младенцам и простым людям — чтобы было понятно Ваничке² и дворнику. А если нет, то ищи, в чем заврался.

*(working English)* "I cannot even say that I am continuing my work — for three days I've written nothing and feel the necessity to change everything and start it all over again. In this work I have seen the danger of abstract reasoning. Before you can look round, you've wandered into a fearful thicket. It seems I stopped in time. There is only one check — accessibility to babes and to simple people — that it be comprehensible to Vanichka² and to the yard-keeper. And if not, then look for where you've talked yourself into nonsense."

**Significance:** the book's stated method and self-correction in one passage. The Vanichka of the test is Tolstoy's own youngest son Ivan ("Vanichka"), who would die four months later (Feb 1895) — lending the formulation a private weight. This is the cornerstone genesis quotation for the dive.

---

### 1894-11-02 — Diary (`v52_153_153_1894_11_02`)
The decision to abandon the catechism form, in the diary.

> Всё верчусь в самом начале. […] Думал тоже о том, что надо бросить катехизичную форму. Утратилось во мне чувство близости Бога […]

*(working English)* "I keep spinning at the very beginning. […] I also thought that the catechism form must be dropped. The sense of God's nearness has been lost in me […]"

**Significance:** the formal turning-point — drop the Q&A "catechism" form — dated to the diary the same day as the matching letter to Chertkov below.

---

### 1894-11-02 — Letter to V. G. Chertkov (`v87_389_noyabrya2`)
The same decision, stated to his chief editor/archivist; also shows the manuscript-custody dynamic.

> О катехизисе, который теперь мне уже хочется писать не в катехизической форме, не могу сказать того же.⁵ Чувствую, что я должен трудиться над этим. И как ни слабы мои силы, продолжаю это дело, и многое смутное становится ясным.

*(working English)* "Of the catechism — which I now want to write not in the catechism form — I cannot say the same.⁵ I feel that I must labour at it. And weak as my powers are, I continue the work, and much that was vague becomes clear."

Also (manuscript-handling, opening of the letter):
> Дубликата, списанный, уничтожьте, a те, которые вам не нужны, пришлите ко мне.¹ Вы, мне думается, грешите слишком большой заботой о сохранности этих писаний.

*(working English)* "Destroy the copied duplicate, and send back to me the ones you don't need.¹ You, I think, sin by too great a care for the preservation of these writings."

**Significance:** the rename decision confirmed to Chertkov; and a candid glimpse of the Chertkov copy-and-preserve apparatus that Tolstoy half-resists.

---

### 1895-02-21 — Diary (`v53_008_010_1895_02_21`)
After Vanichka's death (23 Feb is days away); he can barely call it writing.

> Эти 5 дней поправлял притчи, поправлял Хозяина и Работника и обдумывал, не могу сказать, что писал катехизис. […] Ясно представлялось, как описать ложь, среди которой мы живем, чем она поддерживается и тут же включить то простое миросозерцание, которое я выражаю в катехизисе.

*(working English)* "These five days I corrected the parables, corrected *Master and Man*, and pondered — I cannot say I wrote — the catechism. […] It clearly presented itself to me how to describe the lie we live amidst, what sustains it, and right there to include that simple world-view which I express in the catechism."

**Significance:** the work persists through grief and competing fiction (*Master and Man*); the catechism is the carrier of his «простое миросозерцание».

---

### 1895-03-12 — Diary (`v53_010_013_1895_03_12`)
First entry after Vanichka's death (7 Feb 1895 by the diary's frame). The catechism appears amid the grief and a famous list of unfinished fiction.

> Немного работал над катехизисом. Но только обдумывал. […] 1) Коневская. 2) Кто прав. 3) Отец Сергий. 4) Дьявол в аду. 5) Купон. 6) Записки матери. 7) Александр I. 8) Драма. 9) Переселенцы и башкиры. — Рядом с этим кончать Катехизис.

*(working English)* "Worked a little on the catechism. But only pondered. […] 1) [Hadji-Murat / the Koni story]. 2) Who Is Right. 3) Father Sergius. 4) The Devil in Hell. 5) The Coupon. 6) A Mother's Notes. 7) Alexander I. 8) A Drama. 9) Settlers and Bashkirs. — Alongside these, to finish the Catechism."

**Significance:** the catechism stands at the head of his entire late-period to-do list, set against the fiction backlog — evidence he ranked it co-equal with the novels he never finished.

---

### 1895-03-18 — Diary (`v53_013_013_1895_03_18`)
> По утрам думал над катехизисом. […] Еще был Чертков. Как всегда мне с ним хорошо. И он тоже освежил меня.

*(working English)* "In the mornings I thought over the catechism. […] Chertkov was also here. As always it's good for me with him. And he too refreshed me."

**Significance:** Chertkov physically present during the work; the morning-thinking, no-writing pattern continues.

---

### 1895-12-01 — Letter to V. G. Chertkov (`v87_406_dekabrya1`)
The "start completely from the beginning" struggle, with the title transition («то, чтó было катихизис»).

> Я всеми силами пытаюсь изложить свою веру то, чтó было катихизис — совсем сначала, и то вспыхнет и кажется ясно, то опять потухнет и останавливаюсь.¹ Думаю, что, хотя почти ничего, даже совсем ничего, потому что то, чтó написал, не годится […] думаю, что все таки не грешу, напрягая свои силы на это, хотя бы и без успеха. Чтó-то движется.

*(working English)* "With all my powers I am trying to set out my faith — what was the catechism — completely from the beginning, and now it flares up and seems clear, now it goes out again and I halt.¹ I think that, though it's almost nothing, even nothing at all, because what I've written is no good […] still I think I do not sin in straining my powers at it, even without success. Something is moving."

**Significance:** "what *was* the catechism" — the rename has happened by Dec 1895. The flare-and-fade rhythm and "start from scratch" are now chronic.

---

### 1896-03-06 — Diary (`v53_083_083_1896_03_06`)
> Нынче задумал было — безумие — написать вкратце изложение веры. Разумеется, не вышло.

*(working English)* "Today I conceived — madness — of writing the exposition of faith in brief. Of course it didn't come off."

**Significance:** he now calls it «изложение веры» in the diary too; the dream of a *short* version recurs and fails.

---

### 1896-05-02 — Diary (`v53_083_084_1896_05_02`)
> Писал всё это время Изложение веры. Мало подвинулся. Был Чертков.

*(working English)* "All this time I was writing the Exposition of Faith. Made little progress. Chertkov was here."

**Significance:** steady-state spring 1896; Chertkov again on site. (Same entry records the Spir philosophy reading that feeds the book's metaphysics.)

---

### 1896-06-19 — Diary (`v53_098_099_1896_06_19`)
Records, retrospectively, the **13 June 1896 "finished in rough" milestone** (the diary date 1896-06-13 itself was not written; this is where it surfaces).

> Докончил 13 Июня начерно. Теперь переделывал, но очень мало работаю.

*(working English)* "Finished on 13 June in rough. Now I was reworking it, but I work very little."

**Significance:** pins the first "rough complete" of the *изложение веры* to 13 June 1896 — recovering the milestone the target list flagged as a missing diary date.

---

### 1896-07-19 — Diary (`v53_099_102_1896_07_19`)
The self-judgement that the book has failed its own clarity test — the converse of the Vanichka criterion.

> За это время подвинулся в «Изложении веры». Далеко не то, что нужно и что хочу, совсем недоступно простолюдину и ребенку, но все-таки высказано всё, что знаю, связно и последовательно.

*(working English)* "In this time I advanced in the *Exposition of Faith*. Far from what's needed and what I want — quite inaccessible to the common man and the child — but still, everything I know is stated, coherently and consecutively."

**Significance:** Tolstoy himself admits the work fails the «понятно Ваничке и дворнику» test («совсем недоступно простолюдину и ребенку») — the central tension of the whole project, in his own verdict. (Same entry: the Hadji-Murat thistle vision and the Fet/art passage.)

---

### 1896-09-09 — Letter to S. A. Tolstaya (`v84_654_a9`)
The day-to-day grind on a single passage, written to his wife.

> Я очень занят своей работой, всё бьюсь над одним местом о грехах: вчера как будто уяснил, нынче опять всё искромсал и спутал.⁵ […] Это большое благо иметь дело, в котором не сомневаешься. И у меня есть это счастье.

*(working English)* "I am very busy with my work, still struggling over one passage about the sins: yesterday I seemed to clear it up, today I again hacked it all up and tangled it.⁵ […] It is a great blessing to have work one does not doubt. And I have that happiness."

**Significance:** "the passage about the sins" (грехи) is the book's tripartite scheme (sins / temptations / superstitions); rare granular evidence of which section gave most trouble in Sept 1896.

---

### 1896-09-13 — Letter to V. G. Chertkov (`v87_420_a13`)
Reports the manuscript reaching near-clean form; shows Tanya copying.

> ¹⁰ Большая работа подвигается и теперь даже находится в приблизительно чистом виде.⁹

*(working English)* "¹⁰ The big work is advancing and is now even in approximately clean form.⁹"

(and, on the copy circuit:)
> Таня перепишет и пришлет вам […]

*(working English)* "Tanya will copy it out and send it to you […]"

**Significance:** "the big work" near clean copy; Tatyana L. Tolstaya ("Tanya") confirmed as the working copyist routing texts to Chertkov.

---

### 1896-09-15 — Letter to V. G. Chertkov (`v87_421_a15`)
Chertkov is actively working on the text (editing/copying for circulation).

> Это очень, очень хорошо — то, чтó вы делаете с моим изложением веры.¹ Я очень рад, но только боюсь, что мое то дело все не кончается.

*(working English)* "It is very, very good — what you are doing with my exposition of faith.¹ I am very glad, only I fear that my part of it still won't end."

**Significance:** explicit acknowledgement of Chertkov's editorial hand on «моё изложение веры», alongside Tolstoy's own sense that his work is unfinishable.

---

### 1896-09-26 — Letter to S. A. Tolstaya (`v84_658_a26`)
The completion-in-rough announcement — and the "if I die" framing the prompt flagged.

> прекрасно работал и, кажется, кончил начерно свое изложение веры, по крайней мере так, что если умру, не исправив, всётаки поймут, что я хотел сказать. Теперь хочу писать другое, и начал.

*(working English)* "I worked beautifully and, it seems, finished my exposition of faith in rough — at least so that, if I die without correcting it, they will still understand what I wanted to say. Now I want to write something else, and have begun."

**Significance:** the rough completion, dated 26 Sept 1896 to his wife — nine days before the PSS "rough finished 5 Oct" — with the testamentary "if I die" frame that marks how much weight he placed on it.

---

### 1896-10-10 — Diary (`v53_108_111_1896_10_10`)
The completion entry — and the immediate vow to redo it.

> За это время, хотя в очень дурном виде, но окончил изложение веры. […] Хочу всё изложение веры писать сначала.

*(working English)* "In this time, though in very poor shape, I finished the exposition of faith. […] I want to write the whole exposition of faith over from the beginning."

**Significance:** "finished … in very poor shape" then "want to write it all over again" — the work is declared done and condemned in the same breath. (Rest of entry: the long Spir/Berkeley/Kant idealism meditation that underpins the book's metaphysics of life-as-consciousness.)

---

### 1896-10-12 — Letter to V. G. Chertkov (`v87_423_oktyabrya12`)
The cleanest statement of the "finished one version, started a shorter one, and it's going" cycle — plus same-day copying by Tanya.

> Моя работа все продолжает меня удовлетворять не в том смысле, что хорошо, но что работается. Я кончил в том виде, в котором она теперь, и начал тоже вновь в более простом и сокращенном виде и это пошло.⁶ Нынче написал 8 глав, и Таня, вчера вернувшаяся,⁷ переписала.

*(working English)* "My work continues to satisfy me, not in the sense that it's good, but that it goes. I have finished it in the form it is now, and have begun again in a simpler and shortened form, and this has gone.⁶ Today I wrote 8 chapters, and Tanya, who returned yesterday,⁷ copied them out."

**Significance:** the production rhythm at the very end — finish, then immediately restart shorter; "wrote 8 chapters today, Tanya copied them." Caps the genesis with the two facts the prompt sought: the late shorter-version restart, and Tatyana as same-day copyist.

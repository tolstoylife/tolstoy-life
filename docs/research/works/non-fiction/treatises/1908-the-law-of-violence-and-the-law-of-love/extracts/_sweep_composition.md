# Composition-years witness sweep — «Закон насилия и закон любви» (1908)

Primary diaries, notebooks (Tom 56) and letters (Toms 78, 89) from the composition
window (Jan–Jul 1908). Every Russian quote below is copied verbatim from an extract
file saved in this same `extracts/` directory; the verifying file is named per quote.
Working-English translations are the dive's own, labelled "(working English)".

Provenance note on the corpus: tolstoydigital TEI, extracted with
`extract_tei.py --choice=reg --notes=auto`. The `# bibl` line of each extract gives
the exact PSS т./page. Several genesis facts in the brief came from Serebrovskaya's
editorial apparatus (PSS т.37) — where I could trace them to Tolstoy's own first-hand
text I quote that text; where I could only confirm them via the editorial apparatus
(e.g. Gusev's diary), I flag it explicitly in "Notes/uncertainties".

## Genesis thread (Tolstoy's own words)

**1. 20 Jan 1908 — the start.** Verify against `v56_093_094_1908_01_20.txt` (т.56, p.93–94).
> «Начал писать статью. Об упадке, безверии и непротивлении. Не очень дурно, но слабо.»
(working English) "Began writing an article. On decline, unbelief and non-resistance.
Not very bad, but weak."
The same entry records the people around him that day: «Чертков здесь и пропасть народа...
Абрикосовы, Гусев, Плюснин. Вчера приехал Поша.»

**2. 9 Feb 1908 — "notes for a planned appeal".** Verify against `v56_098_104_1908_01_09.txt`
(the entry's `[opener]` is «9 Февр. 1908»; т.56, p.98–104; the appeal sentence sits at p.99).
> «Все это — заметки для задуманного воззвания.»
(working English) "All this is notes for the planned appeal/proclamation."
Tolstoy repeatedly calls the work-in-progress a «воззвание» (appeal/proclamation), not yet
an "article" with the final title.

**3. 9 Mar 1908 (Notebook №1) — the article won't move.** Verify against
`v56_299_301_Zapisnaja_knizhka_1908_g_No_1_janvar_mart.txt` (т.56, p.299–301; the brief cites p.301).
> «Статья плохо подвигается. Думается об описании убийц революционеров, палачей и правительства.»
(working English) "The article advances badly. I keep thinking about a description of the
killers — revolutionaries, executioners, and the government."

**4. 10 Mar 1908 — won't go, but won't abandon.** Verify against `v56_109_110_1908_03_10.txt`
(т.56, p.109–110).
> «Занят был за письменным столом статьей. Не идет, а не хочется оставить.»
(working English) "I was busy at the writing-table with the article. It won't go, yet I don't
want to leave it."

**5. 18 Mar 1908 (Notebook №2) — wants to abandon it.** Verify against
`v56_302_346_Zapisnaja_knizhka_1908_g_No_2_janvar_ijun.txt` (т.56, p.302–346; under the «18 Мар.»
dateline; the brief cites p.321).
> «Хочется бросить начатую статью, а хорошо бы написать тоже для народа.»
(working English) "I want to drop the article I've begun, though it would be good to write
one for the common people as well."

**6. 21 Mar 1908 — it cleared up.** Verify against `v56_110_111_1908_03_21.txt` (т.56, p.110–111).
> «...несмотря на нездоровье, писал статью очень хорошо. Все, что было неясно, уяснилось, и
> гуляя думал, и кажется все ясно, и допишу.»
(working English) "...despite ill-health, wrote the article very well. Everything that had been
unclear cleared up, and walking I thought it over, and it all seems clear, and I shall finish it."

**7. 12 May 1908 — finished, and he liked it.** Verify against `v56_117_117_1908_05_12.txt`
(т.56, p.117). NB: in the diary text this sentence is a footnote-tail (marked ¹) appended to the
12 May entry.
> «Прочел местами свою работу Закон Насилия и Закон Любви, и мне понравилось, и я кончил ее.»
(working English) "Read parts of my work The Law of Violence and the Law of Love, and I liked it,
and I have finished it."
CORRECTION to the brief's genesis fact: the diary reads «и мне **понравилось**» (neuter), not
«понравилась». The brief's «мне понравилась, и я кончил ее» does not match the primary text on the
verb form. The completion clause «и я кончил ее» is verbatim. The same entry ties the finishing to
news of executions: «Вчера мне было особенно мучительно тяжело от известия о 20 повешенных крестьянах.»

**8. 10 Jun 1908 — handed out to be copied fair.** Verify against `v56_133_133_1908_06_10.txt`
(т.56, p.133).
> «Отдал переписывать большую статью.»
(working English) "Gave the big article out to be copied fair."

**9. 17 Jun 1908 — took the big article up again.** Verify against `v56_134_134_1908_06_17.txt`
(т.56, p.134).
> «Написал за это время статейку о приговоре Молочникова и занялся опять большой статьей. — Вчера
> писал, и нынче очень хорошо — так кажется — написал о непротивлении и вообще исправил.»
(working English) "In this time I wrote a little piece about the Molochnikov sentence and took up
the big article again. — Yesterday I wrote, and today very well — so it seems — wrote about
non-resistance and corrected it generally."
This is first-hand evidence that the article kept being revised through mid-June, after the
12 May "finished".

**10. Notebook №3 — "insert a chapter on religious understanding".** Verify against
`v56_347_361_Zapisnaja_knizhka_1908_g_No_3_fevralja_avgust.txt` (т.56, p.347–361). The very next
line in the notebook is the dateline «[16 Іюня].»
> «Закон насилия и закон любви. Вставить главу о религиозном понимании.»
(working English) "The Law of Violence and the Law of Love. Insert a chapter on religious
understanding."
First-hand evidence of structural reworking of the chapters in June, under the final title.

## Notes / uncertainties

- **«чепуха» is NOT first-hand Tolstoy.** The brief's 21/23 Feb «чепуха» lines come from
  **N. N. Gusev's diary**, quoted inside Serebrovskaya's editorial History of the Text
  (`v37_436_438_Zakon_nasilija_i_zakon_ljubvi_Istorija_p.txt`, lines ~15). Tolstoy's own diary
  entries for 21/22/23 Feb (`v56_111_112_1908_02_21.txt`, `v56_108_108_1908_02_22.txt`,
  `v56_108_108_1908_02_23.txt`) do **not** mention the article at all. The wording «Теперь я буду
  свою чепуху кончать... Хотя она и чепуха, но она так захватила меня» is Gusev reporting Tolstoy's
  speech, not a Tolstoy autograph. Quote it as "Gusev's diary, via PSS т.37 editorial apparatus."

- **First-title "Всему бывает конец".** Confirmed via the editorial apparatus only
  (`v37_436_438...` and the editorial notes in `v89_800_fevralya10.xml`), not found as a Tolstoy
  autograph title in the extracted diary/notebook bodies for this window. The 20 Jan diary names the
  subject («Об упадке, безверии и непротивлении») but not the title «Всему бывает конец».

- **"Remove chapter III" (16 June).** Not found verbatim in the primary diaries/notebooks. The
  closest first-hand trace is Notebook №3's «Закон насилия и закон любви. Вставить главу о
  религиозном понимании.» immediately followed by the «[16 Іюня]» dateline
  (`v56_347_361_..._fevralja_avgust.txt`). The specific "removing ch. III" wording is Gusev-via-
  Serebrovskaya, not corroborated first-hand here — flag if used.

- **12 May verb form.** Diary reads «и мне понравилось» (neuter), correcting the brief's
  «понравилась». The completion clause «и я кончил ее» is verbatim. The diary text places the whole
  sentence as a footnote-tail (¹) on the 12 May entry; `--notes=auto` recovered it.

- **Date-vs-filename caution.** Several 1908 diary filenames carry a suffix that mismatches the
  entry's own dateline (e.g. `v56_098_104_1908_01_09.xml` opens «9 Февр. 1908», i.e. it is the
  9 Feb entry running pp.98–104, not 9 Jan). I have used each extract's `[opener]` dateline and
  `# bibl` page, not the filename suffix, for dating. Trust the in-file dateline.

- All page numbers above are from each extract's `# bibl` line (PSS т.56 for diaries/notebooks;
  т.78 / т.89 for letters), which I verified at first hand in the saved extracts.

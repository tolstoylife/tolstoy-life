## Not openly available / where to request

### N.N. Gusev — portrait photograph (period, 1907–1909)

Nikolai Nikolaevich Gusev (1882–1967) was Tolstoy's personal secretary
1907–1909 and the diary-witness to the composition of «Закон насилия и
закон любви». A period photograph of Tolstoy with Gusev at Yasnaya
Polyana (1909) is referenced in secondary sources and on Pinterest
(pin/442619469650448306) but no freely-licensed version was located on
Wikimedia Commons. The Commons file `File:Gusevnn.jpg` (CC-BY-SA 4.0,
credited "Durmanov", dated 1950) depicts the chess player of the same
name, not the literary scholar/secretary.

**Where to request:** State Museum of L.N. Tolstoy, Moscow
(tolstoymuseum.ru / photo archive); Yasnaya Polyana estate archive.
The tolstoy-manuscript.ru site has a Gusev biographical entry
(id=48, catid=3) but the page returned HTTP 401 — may require
institutional login.

### Manuscript facsimile — «Закон насилия и закон любви» рук. № 58

The final manuscript (рук. № 58) is one of 1,536 pages held in the
State Museum of L.N. Tolstoy, Moscow. The «Все Толстой в один клик»
digitisation project (State Tolstoy Museum + Yasnaya Polyana + ABBYY)
covers many manuscripts, but no openly-licensed facsimile page for
this specific work was located via Wikimedia Commons or public search.

**Where to request:** State Museum of L.N. Tolstoy, Moscow —
рукописный отдел; also queryable via Goskatalog
(web.goskatalog.ru — SSL errors prevented direct access on
2026-06-08; retry via browser). The ABBYY "All of Tolstoy" portal
may hold digitised manuscript pages behind an institutional gate.

### «Свободное слово» (Christchurch, 1909) — title page

The first complete Russian-language edition, published by
V.G. Chertkov's Svobodnoe Slovo press in Christchurch, England, 1909.
Translator/editor: V. Tchertkoff. An Internet Archive item
(details/LawOfLoveAndLawOfViolence_LevTolstoy) exists but was
inaccessible due to SSL errors on 2026-06-08; the item likely
contains a title-page scan.

**Where to retry:** https://archive.org/details/LawOfLoveAndLawOfViolence_LevTolstoy
(access directly in browser; the SSL error was tool-side, not
site-wide). Also: imwerden.de and vtoraya-literatura.com — neither
returned a direct hit for this title in the 2026-06-08 sweep; a
manual search on both sites for "закон насилия" is warranted.

### Library of Congress — Prokudin-Gorsky glass-plate originals

The LOC holds the original glass-plate negatives for the
23 May 1908 Tolstoy colour session (digital ID prok.01971).

available via the LOC Prints & Photographs Online Catalog

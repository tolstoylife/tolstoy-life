## Manuscripts and proofs

**Archival provenance note (from PSS vol. 33 apparatus and tolstoy-lit.ru):**
Manuscripts and proofs for *Resurrection* are distributed across four institutions:
- **ГМТ** (Государственный музей Толстого, Moscow): папки 1388, 4939 (main collection)
  and папки 14, 43–45, 49–58 (Chertkov archive, now part of ГМТ)
- **ИРЛИ** (Институт русской литературы / Pushkin House, St Petersburg): catalogue no. 10402
- **РГБ** (Российская государственная библиотека, formerly Lenin Library): фонд XXX
- Scattered private holdings (early 20th-c., partly absorbed into above)
Total: >8,000 manuscript sheets across 24+ numbered manuscript units and multiple proof sets.

## Access notes and next steps

**To obtain usable reproductions:**
1. **ГМТ Pasternak illustrations** (RU-ILL-01 to RU-ILL-09): Contact Государственный музей Л.Н. Толстого, Prechistenka 11, Moscow. Reproduction requests via the museum's scientific department. The КП numbers above are the authoritative identifiers to cite.
2. **ГМТ manuscripts and proofs** (RU-MS-01 to RU-MS-03): Research reading room at Prechistenka 11. Manuscripts held in the "steel room"; advance appointment required. Cite папки 1388/4939 (main collection) or папки 14/43–58 (Chertkov archive) for the proofs.
3. **ГРМ portraits** (RU-PRT-03, RU-PRT-04 bronze, RU-PRT-06): Virtual Russian Museum portal (rusmuseumvrm.ru) has viewable images; reproduction rights via ГРМ photo library.
4. **Нива 1899 digital facsimile**: East View full-archive subscription at eivis.ru/browse/udb/2350 (SHPL proxy). Государственная публичная историческая библиотека (SHPL), Moscow, provides remote proxy access to registered users.
5. **Goskatalog** (web.goskatalog.ru): Portal timed out during this sweep (JS-heavy). The above KAMIS inventory numbers (КП-) are the correct identifiers to search in Goskatalog if direct access is needed. Suggested search: museum = "Государственный музей Л.Н. Толстого", КП prefix numbers above.

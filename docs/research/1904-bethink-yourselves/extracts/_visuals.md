## What is NOT openly available / where to request it

- **Tolstoy 1904 specifically (Yasnaya Polyana):** The Commons category
  `Category:Leo_Tolstoy_in_1904` was empty at query time. The primary
  holding of Chertkov-era photographs (1900–1910) is the **State Museum of
  L. N. Tolstoy, Moscow** (Государственный музей Л. Н. Толстого,
  ul. Prechistenka 11) and **RGALI fond 508** (Tolstoy family papers).
  For reproduction rights contact: gtm@tolstoymuseum.ru. The Tolstoy
  Digital project (tolstoydigital.net) links to some photographs but does
  not host high-resolution downloads.

- **Chertkov 1903–1905 portrait:** RGALI fond 552 (Chertkov papers) and the
  British Library Chertkov collection (Add MS series). Contact:
  manuscripts@bl.uk for BL reproductions.

- **Free Age Press pamphlet title page:** No openly licensed scan found on
  Commons or Internet Archive at query time. The British Library holds
  multiple Free Age Press publications; the National Library of Scotland
  (NLS) and Bodleian also have holdings. IA identifier for the English
  Bethink Yourselves! text is pg27189 (Project Gutenberg), but the PG file
  does not include a cover scan.

- **The Times first printing (June 1904):** The Times Digital Archive is
  paywalled (Gale). The British Newspaper Archive (britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk)
  may have the pages but requires a subscription and would be rights-reserved
  for reproduction.

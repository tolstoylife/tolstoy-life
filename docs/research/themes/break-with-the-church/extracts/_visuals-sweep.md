## IMAGES LOCATED BUT NOT DOWNLOADABLE / NOT YET ON COMMONS

### A. «Определение Святейшего Синода» (Synod edict, Церковные ведомости, 24 Feb 1901)

Facsimile of the printed edict as it appeared in the official Synod gazette. No PD facsimile scan was found on Commons or archive.org during this sweep. The Российская государственная библиотека (RGB) holds runs of Церковные ведомости; the Государственный архив (РГИА) holds the original signed document.

### B. Caricatures / popular prints of the excommunication

No Commons images with clear attribution to a specific 1901 excommunication caricature were found (searches on "excommunication synod caricature", "lubok Tolstoy synod", "Bogatov" all returned empty). The well-known satirical prints circulating after 1905 press liberalisation are documented in secondary literature but appear not to be on Commons. The State Historical Museum (ГИМ) and the Russian National Library graphic collections are the likely holdings.

### C. Repin — "Tolstoy na pashne" / 1901 variant (if distinct from 1887 ploughing)

The 1887 "Tolstoy ploughing" (Tretyakov) is confirmed downloaded. A separate 1901 "Tolstoy na pashne" oil is sometimes cited as also by Repin but Commons search did not surface a distinct file beyond the 1887 Tretyakov work. If a separate 1901 "na pashne" canvas exists, it may be in the Russian Museum or a private collection.

### D. Tolstoy's reply to the Synod — handwritten or first-print facsimile

No image facsimile of Tolstoy's "Ответ на Определение Синода" as first printed (Свободное слово, Christchurch, 1901) was found. The Tolstoy Museum archive (tolstoy-iss.kamiscloud.ru) and Chertkov papers at the RGB are the likely locations.

### E. Свободное слово (Free Word) press — title pages of 1901 pamphlets

Chertkov's Christchurch (England) pamphlets — "Ответ Синоду", "Что такое религия", "К духовенству" — are documented in the archive.org Chertkov/Tolstoy corpus but without separate cover images. Gorky's 1902 Gaspra visit is documented in his "Reminiscences of Tolstoy" (1920) but no photograph of the meeting surfaced. The Gorky archive (РГАЛИ) is the likely holding.

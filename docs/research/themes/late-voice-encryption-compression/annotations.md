**Translation:** "It won't get through [the censor] in Russia, but it can be printed abroad." ## Annotation 2 — translation requested (index, Chertkov table, `v89_757`)

> «печатать всю или ничего»

**Translation:** "to print the whole thing or nothing." Full sentence: «я поставил conditio sine qua non — печатать всю или ничего» = "I set it as a *conditio sine qua non* — to print the whole [article] or nothing" (the "whole" is feminine, agreeing with «статью», the article «Обращение к правительству…»). In `dossier.yaml` as `chan-all-or-nothing`; the **index table row leaves it untranslated** — fix in the same pass.

**New §1.1 row.** Also previously unknown to this research project. Two open questions:

1. **The 243-page count is anomalously small** for a "full Vol I" — the 1921 Berlin Vol I is 576 pp, the 1911 Кушнерёв is 544 pp, the 1906 first edition was published as one volume in two parts. The 1923 Госиздат at 243 pp likely covers only **part of Vol I's scope** (perhaps a single tom in a multi-tom Soviet reprint where Vol I was split into more physical volumes than the first edition), or possibly the **condensed Краткая биография** tradition (§1.1's 1908 / 1912 condensations) — but the title "Биография Льва Николаевича Толстого" without "Краткая" argues against the condensed interpretation. Disambiguation needed against the actual title-page surface (next session, when the Presidential Library record's image surface is captured).
2. **Probable explanation for the rumoured "1923 Berlin reissue" (§1.8).** The original WebSearch hit from session 2 that flagged a "1923 Berlin Vols I–III reissue" appears to have been a misattribution of this 1923 Госиздат Moscow + Petrograd 3rd edition. The §1.8 open question can be downgraded from "unverified search hit" to "candidate-for-retirement, pending one final WorldCat / German-national-library check to rule out a parallel 1923 Berlin event."

## Side-finds (small refinements to existing sections)

### §1.7 — two independent digital surrogates of the 2000 «Алгоритм» print

The 2000 Алгоритм reprint Book II (Vols III + IV) is digitised twice, independently:
- **az.lib.ru** — OCR by Виталий Адаменко, 10–20 January 2003. Underlying `text_1922_tolstoy06.shtml` (and `text_1905_tolstoy05.shtml` for Vol III, which is from Book I).
- **imwerden.de** ([publ-16339](https://imwerden.de/publ-16339)) — electronic edition by Andrey Nikitin-Perensky and Stanislav Lvovskiy, 658 pp, OCR PDF 33.4 MB, ISBN 5-888-78017-0 (= the Алгоритм 2000 ISBN itself, so this is the same print run).

The current §1.7 mentions only the az.lib.ru chain. Refinement to note both digital descendants is factually clean but not on the verdict's critical path.

### §2.1 — Erik Nordenström biographical clarification

Litteraturbanken's Svenskt översättarlexikon does not have a dedicated Nordenström article, but its article on translator Sören Rydström (https://litteraturbanken.se/översättarlexikon/artiklar/Sören_Rydström) mentions Nordenström once, in this passage:

> *"Trots goda ryskkunskaper hade han dessutom sneglat på artillerikaptenen Erik Gustaf Nordenströms högst diskutabla översättning från 1910 och inhämtat en del vändningar därifrån."*

Working English: "Despite good Russian skills, he also glanced at the artillery captain Erik Gustaf Nordenström's highly questionable translation from 1910 and adopted some turns of phrase from it."

Two facts transfer to §2.1:
- **Erik Nordenström's profession:** artillery captain. Filling in the Swedish translator's social position.
- The "1910 highly questionable" reference does **NOT** apply to the Birukoff translation — the Rydström article's context is Gogol's *Döda själar*, not Tolstoy's biography. §2.1 should record the biographical fact without inheriting the quality judgement.

### §1.1 Swedish year-range confirmation

Erik Oskarsson Antikvariat (Lund), via a Google snippet: *"Leo Tolstoj. Hans lif och hans verk. Själfbiografiska bref och anteckningar samt biografiska uppgifter. I-II · Stockholm, P A Norstedt & Söner. 1906-1909."* — confirms the Swedish Norstedt edition publication-year range as 1906–1909, three häften across two Russian-volume scopes (Första delen 1 + 2 covers Russian Vol I, Andra delen covers Russian Vol II).

## What this session was, and why this handoff exists

The original session was supposed to execute TODO §8 Step 1 — the **ch18 probe** for Biryukov Vol III: confirm `az.lib.ru` carries a Vol III file, capture ch18 in the Vol IV style, RU↔RU diff against the current `tolstoy-lit.ru` capture, report the gap.

The URL probe **succeeded** (URL: `http://az.lib.ru/b/birjukow_p_i/text_1905_tolstoy05.shtml`) and the edition check **passed** (both sources close with Biryukov's "28 октября 1915 г." dateline — same 1915 first edition). But three things surfaced that turned a probe into a research question Johan wants properly answered before the recapture work continues:

1. **The az.lib.ru file isn't an OCR of the 1915 print directly.** Its own front-matter cites: *"Изд: Павел Бирюков. Биография Л. Н. Толстого, книга вторая (Серия 'Гений в искусстве'), М., 'Алгоритм', 2000; OCR: Адаменко Виталий (adamenko77@mail.ru)"*. So the chain is **1915 print → 2000 «Алгоритм» Moscow reprint → 2003 Adamenko OCR → az.lib.ru HTML**. Same chain for Vol IV (also marked "книга вторая" — the Алгоритм reprint was a two-book set). Vol IV's known typesetter-error correction note is in this file too, and the OCR'er flags one in Vol III ch5/6.
2. **Biryukov himself said Vol III is unfinished.** From the 1915 second-layer preface (Onex près Genève, 1 November 1915): *"Вполне сознаю, что собранный мною, координированный и комментированный материал преподносится читателю почти в сыром виде. На более тщательную обработку его у меня не хватило сил. Пусть сделают это другие."* ("I am fully aware that the material I have collected, coordinated and annotated is presented to the reader in almost raw form. I did not have the strength for more thorough processing. Let others do it.") The edition-preference question is therefore not trivial: Biryukov publicly invited revision.
3. **Johan owns a Swedish authorised edition examined by Tolstoy himself.** Title page: *"Leo Tolstoj — Hans lif och hans verk. Själfbiografiska bref och anteckningar samt biografiska uppgifter samlade af Paul Birukoff samt granskade af Leo Tolstoj. Bemyndigad öfversättning från ryska originalet af Erik Nordenström. Andra delen. Stockholm, P. A. Norstedt & Söners förlag."* Three books in his possession: Första delen 1, Första delen 2, Andra delen. The Slutord (page 453) closes at 8 December 1884 and is signed *"Kostroma. Byn Ivanskoje, den 27 augusti 1908. P. Birukoff."* The Slutord forward-flags Vol III: *"för den följande, tredje delen af biografien, komma äfven mina egna personliga minnen att ligga till grund..."* This is a witness to **the biography in its pre-Tolstoy-death, three-volume conception, authorised by Tolstoy in his lifetime** — primary-source weight that has not yet been factored into the existing Vol III/IV work.

The probe is paused. The mechanical ch18 capture-and-diff still has to happen, but it should happen *after* the edition-preference verdict, not before, because the verdict may move the source choice off the 1915 az.lib.ru file.

## What the previous session *did not* settle (the work this project has)

Five buckets, in roughly the order they should be tackled:

### 1. Russian editions: complete bibliographic chain

Confirm and document, with publisher / city / year / pagination / preface variants:

- Vol I first edition (1906 — Moscow? Sytin? Posrednik? two parts or one?)
- Vol II first edition (1908)
- Vol III first edition (1915 — confirmed Onex/Geneva-signed, but who *printed* it? Sytin Moscow? Posrednik? An émigré press given Biryukov was in Switzerland?)
- Vol IV first edition (1922 — Berlin, publisher? Ladyzhnikov? Grzhebin? Another?)
- The 1911-1913 expanded/corrected Russian editions one search hit mentioned ("expanded and corrected in further editions of 1911-3 and 1921-3") — locate, confirm scope of revisions
- 1921 Berlin Ladyzhnikov 3-volume reissue (Egorov's auction confirms existence; need to know whether it was just a reprint of pre-revolutionary Vols 1-3, or whether Biryukov revised them in exile)
- The 1922-1923 Berlin 4-volume set that the search result alluded to (separate from, or same as, the Ladyzhnikov three-volume one? Were Vols 1-3 reissued in 1922-23 to match the 1922 Vol IV?)
- 2000 «Алгоритм» Moscow «Гений в искусстве» reprint (two-book format — confirm which physical edition it reproduces; the editor's introduction should say)
- Any later Russian reissues (litres ebook, DirectMedia)

For each: was Biryukov involved (alive until 1931, in Geneva)? Did he authorise it? Did he revise?

### 2. Translations: catalogue and weight

For each known translation, identify: translator, publisher, city, year(s), number of volumes/parts, what Russian source edition it translates from, authorisation status, whether Tolstoy reviewed it (only possible pre-1910), and any modern critical assessment of its quality.

Known starting points:
- **Swedish — Erik Nordenström, P. A. Norstedt & Söners, Stockholm.** Three books in Johan's possession (Första delen 1, Första delen 2, Andra delen). Andra delen Slutord dated 27 August 1908, closes at 8 December 1884. Title page explicitly states *"granskade af Leo Tolstoj"*. **A fourth Swedish volume (Tredje delen) may or may not exist** — Tolstoy died November 1910, so a Russian Vol III equivalent in Swedish would have been a post-mortem translation; was it ever made?
- **German — Eugen Diederichs, Jena.** Likely covers Vols 1-3 (1906/08/11). Translator? Authorisation?
- **English — "The Life of Tolstoy", Internet Archive `lifeoftolstoy00biriuoft`.** Probably Aylmer Maude single-volume abridgement; confirm and identify any other English-language editions.
- **Dutch — Project Gutenberg 20128.** Translator? Source edition?
- **Any French, Italian, Czech, Polish, Yiddish editions** — Biryukov's biography was internationally significant; the catalogue should be exhaustive within reason.

### 3. Tolstoy's own review involvement: scope and dates

The Swedish title-page claim *"granskade af Leo Tolstoj"* and various secondary sources' references to "personal consultation with Tolstoy" and "authorised biography" need to be pinned down concretely:

- Through what date did Tolstoy review the manuscript? (He died November 1910; the Russian Vol III preface was begun July 1909 from Ivanovskoye, near where Tolstoy lived at Yasnaya Polyana — so review through 1909 is plausible.)
- Which volumes/sections did he actually examine? The Swedish edition's Första-Andra delen are translated from Russian originals through 1908; was the Russian source manuscript shown to Tolstoy before Russian publication, or only after?
- What did Tolstoy think of the result? (His diaries, correspondence, and the Yasnaya Polyana visitor records should have entries.)
- What's the implication for citation? If Biryukov's "raw form" admission (1915) covers only Vol III, then Vols 1-2 may carry a stronger Tolstoy-reviewed authority than Vol III, which Biryukov drafted alone in Swiss exile from 1909-1915 with Tolstoy already dead.

### 4. Quality assessment per edition / translation

For each entry in §1 and §2, an honest quality note: print runs, known OCR quality (the 2000 → 2003 → az.lib.ru chain has surfaced one ch5/6 typesetter error already), digitisation provenance, completeness (any abridgements? footnote handling? embedded primary documents present?). This is where the existing Vol IV index pattern (`docs/research/doukhobors/biryukov-vol4/index.md` — confirms az.lib.ru's advantage over tolstoy-lit.ru) is the model to follow per volume per edition.

The Swedish edition in Johan's possession should be quality-assessed *as a translation* (against the Russian) and *as a witness* (does it preserve material the Russian 1915 capture lost?). The 1908 endpoint and pre-death authorisation make it a check on whether the post-1910 Russian rewrites of earlier volumes introduced editorial shifts.

### 5. Preferred-edition verdict

The pointed question the §8 recapture work needs answered:

**Which edition should be the citation reference for Biryukov's Vol III going forward — the 1915 first edition (currently on az.lib.ru via the 2000 reprint), or some later authorial revision?**

Specifically:
- If Biryukov revised Vol III between 1915 and his 1931 death (most plausibly in a 1921-1923 Berlin reissue), that revised text supersedes 1915.
- If he did not revise, the 1915 first edition is the authorial last word and the az.lib.ru text is fine to recapture from.
- If the 1921 Ladyzhnikov 3-vol set was a pure reprint of pre-revolutionary Russian volumes, that doesn't help; we need to know whether a Berlin Vol III in the 1922-1923 frame was revised.

The verdict should be specific enough to either greenlight TODO §8 Step 2 (recapture from `text_1905_tolstoy05.shtml`) or to redirect it to a different source (a scan of a Berlin Vol III, a wikisource transcription, an interlibrary-loan request, etc.). The same verdict logic should be applied to Vol IV (was the 1922 Berlin first edition Biryukov's last word, or did he correct it in subsequent reissues?), since the Vol IV translation is now done and would only be revisited under strong evidence.

### Bonus: implication for TODO §9 (Vol I / Vol II audit)

Whichever edition wins the verdict for Vol III, the same logic applies to Vols 1-2. The Swedish edition Johan owns provides a Tolstoy-reviewed independent witness for Vols 1-2 specifically — that's a real asset for the §9 work, possibly more valuable than the az.lib.ru 1905-edition capture, since the Swedish was made when Tolstoy was alive.

# P. I. Biryukov, *Biography of L. N. Tolstoy* — Volume IV

A complete capture of the Russian text of **Volume IV** of Pavel Ivanovich
Biryukov's authorised biography of Tolstoy, «Биография Л. Н. Толстого», as
digitized on [az.lib.ru](http://az.lib.ru) (Lib.ru/Классика). The volume covers
**1900–1910** — the Boer War and the renewed Doukhobor question, the 1901
excommunication, the Crimean illness, the Russo-Japanese War, the 1905
revolution, *I Cannot Be Silent*, the 1908 jubilee, the secret will, and finally
Tolstoy's flight from Yasnaya Polyana and his death at Astapovo. It is the
**final volume** of the four; with it the project holds Biryukov's biography
whole.

Biryukov (1860–1931) was one of Tolstoy's closest disciples and an eyewitness to
much of what he describes; the biography quotes Tolstoy's letters and diaries at
length, so it doubles as a primary source. Volume IV also embeds several primary
documents in full — Tolstoy's appeals and his will (see below). The work is
**public domain** (the author died in 1931).

This folder is the Russian source base for a full English translation, mirroring
the layout of [Volume III](../biryukov-vol3/index.md). Translation into `en/` is
a later, separate job; this capture is the working Russian text.

## Source & method

- **Source:** `http://az.lib.ru/b/birjukow_p_i/text_1922_tolstoy06.shtml` — the
  whole volume on a single page (≈930 KB).
- **Provenance:** OCR by Vitaly Adamenko (2003) from the «Алгоритм» (Moscow, 2000)
  reprint — issued there as *Биография Л. Н. Толстого, книга вторая* in the
  series «Гений в искусстве» — of Biryukov's **1922** Volume IV. So the text is
  the 1922 biography, two removes from the original print (2000 reprint → 2003
  OCR).
- **Encoding:** the page is served in **windows-1251**; decoded to UTF-8 on
  capture.
- **Why az.lib.ru and not tolstoy-lit.ru:** the az text is a single clean file
  with no hex-encoded JavaScript interpolations (the trap that silently dropped
  text in the Vol III scrape from tolstoy-lit.ru). It also carries ~4.5% more
  text — the embedded primary documents and the author's asterisk footnotes,
  which the other reproduction renders thinly or drops.
- **Footnotes:** Biryukov's footnotes are kept in the source's own asterisk
  style — an inline `(*)` marker and the note as `(* … *)` (18 notes; 19 inline
  markers, one without a matching note in the source itself).
- **Stripped:** the az.lib.ru title page, the OCR colophon, and the site's
  navigation/rating widgets. Biryukov's text, his footnotes, Tolstoy's quoted
  insertions, and the embedded primary documents are kept.
- **Headings:** each chapter file carries the chapter title as an `# H1`; the
  four part-dividers (*Часть 1–IV*) appear as `## H2` at the chapters where they
  open; the embedded primary documents appear as `## H2` inline within the
  chapter where they sit in the print.

> **Caveat.** This is a digitized reproduction (a 2003 OCR of a 2000 reprint), not
> a critical edition. The digitizer noted that he corrected an obvious
> typesetter's error in Chapter 15 and flagged the foreign-language fragments for
> spell-checking. For citation against the printed biography, cross-check a
> passage against `az.lib.ru`, `ru.wikisource.org`, or a scan of the original
> 1922 print volume.

# Thread-map — the ENCODED FORM dives (encryption & compression)

Synthesis-first mining of eleven already-completed sibling dives for the late-voice theme
dive (`late-voice-encryption-compression`). This file maps the **encoded** half of the
corpus surface: the народный рассказ / parable / legend / fiction / drama works whose form
served concealment vs directness, plus the compression-arc datapoints (the anthology series
and the distilled aphorism).

**Two threads.**
- **Thread 1 — encryption under censorship (LEADS):** which truths the late Tolstoy encoded
  because he could not state them in legal Russian print, and how genre + channel served
  concealment vs directness. Smoking-gun = any moment Tolstoy names the constraint or the
  workaround.
- **Thread 2 — compression of the late metaphysics:** the worldview (unity of all life /
  «это ты», non-resistance, rejection of church-and-state) distilling argument → story →
  aphorism, with the anthology series as endpoint (Мысли мудрых людей 1903 → Круг чтения
  1906 → На каждый день 1909 → Путь жизни 1910).

**Reuse rule.** Every row cites the sibling dive's own `evidenceRef`; the `extract` paths
below are rewritten RELATIVE TO THIS FILE as `../../<dive-slug>/extracts/<file>` — reuse
without re-extracting. Russian is verbatim (byte-faithful in the named sibling extract);
English is labelled "(working English)".

**Genre poles:** parable/legend/folk-tale/fiction/drama (encoded) ↔ treatise (direct).
**Channel poles:** legal Russian print (Posrednik / journal / Collected Works) ↔ Chertkov's
«Свободное слово» abroad / abroad-first ↔ posthumous ↔ withheld/secret.

## PART 1 — Per-dive sections (marquee finding + genre/channel classification)

### 1. `1903-folk-tales` — THE KEYSTONE (народный рассказ as encrypted forbidden truth)

**Marquee (encryption).** The five 1903 tales are *the encrypted form of a political truth
Tolstoy was forbidden to print.* On 6 May 1903 he told Sholom Aleichem that the Kishinev
pogrom's true culprit is «одно правительство» — the government — *«этого-то я не могу сказать
в русском легальном издании».* What he could not say as statement he published as legend.
The cluster is simultaneously the most compressed statement of his unity-of-life metaphysics
(«это ты» / tat tvam asi) — so this single dive carries BOTH threads at once.

**Genre:** parable / legend (народный рассказ; «Разрушение ада» = satirical legend).
**Channel:** split across all poles — Yiddish almanac first («Гилф», Warsaw); legal Russian
Posrednik (Асархадон, Три вопроса 1903); banned-then-Russia-1906 (Труд, смерть и болезнь;
Это ты); abroad-only via «Свободное слово» / Russia-1917 (Разрушение ада). The full channel
spectrum in one cluster.

**Keystone evidence — quoted in full (the seed of the whole theme):**

> **`1903-folk-tales:E03`** (letter, PSS Tom 74 pp.118–119, to S. N. Rabinovich / Sholom
> Aleichem, 1903-05-06)
> RU (verbatim): «виновник не только кишиневских ужасов, но всего того разлада, который
> поселяется в некоторой малой части — и не народной — русского населения[…]одно
> правительство. К сожалению, этого-то я не могу сказать в русском легальном издании.»
> EN (working English): "the culprit not only of the Kishinev horrors but of all that
> discord which settles into a certain small — and not popular — part of the Russian
> population … is the government alone. Unfortunately, this is just what I cannot say in a
> legal Russian publication."
> Extract: `../../1903-folk-tales/extracts/v74_144_S_H_RabinovichuSHolom_Alejxemu.txt`
> Significance: the political core, and the reason the answer must come as coded legend, not
> statement — the censorship constraint that shapes the form. **This is row E03 named in the
> dive plan.**

Its neighbours E10 (the third tale held back «из боязни, что она будет запрещена»), E12 (the
fourth contribution «Это ты»), E20–E21 (the devil naming the Church his own invention — «и
тогда я выдумал церковь» — the line that made «Разрушение ада» unprintable in Russia until
1917), E23 (Russia-1917 only) and the metaphysics keystones E15–E17 are all in the table below.

### 2. `stories-for-the-people` — the genre's origin (народные рассказы 1881–87)

**Marquee.** The genre-set itself: moral parables for the popular reader, the lived practice
of "art for the people" a decade before What Is Art? theorised it. Carried by Posrednik
(kopeck booklets, 1–1½ kop.). Censorship was *the daily weather* — «Всё запрещают» (E29);
several tales arrested in the Feb 1887 wave (E30, E32) or condemned by the spiritual censor
as the most «безбожной» book it knew (E33). Bookend: the 24 Oct 1910 grading letter (E40),
two weeks before death — Tolstoy ranks his own народные рассказы into four tiers (the WIA
footnote vs the terminal verdict).

**Genre:** parable / folk-tale / legend (the origin set). The Цветник preface (E09–E10)
states the genre *licence*: angels, devils, walking on water are admissible-as-truth because
the criterion is moral, not documentary.
**Channel:** legal Russian Posrednik (cheap editions; «как можно дешевле») — but heavily
censored; some tales («Крестник») printed only in 1906.

### 3. `1886-the-power-of-darkness` — drama, stage ban 1887–95

**Marquee.** The chain-of-sin tragedy in peasant speech, written FOR the people's stage —
yet the first peasant audience did not understand it (E `com-peasants-not-understood`), and
by Jan 1887 Tolstoy frames its moral target as the educated «большой свет» («for whom I wrote
it», `let-great-world`). Folk form, "great world" audience — the marquee complication.

**Genre:** drama (folk tragedy). **Channel split = the dive's gift to the theme:** print
*free* (Posrednik, 100,000+ copies, Feb 1887) but the *stage* banned. Alexander III first
praised it («Чудная вещь»), then Pobedonostsev's "cruder than Zola" letter reversed him
(`com-pobedonostsev-zola` / `com-aleks3-reversal`) — stage ban held 1887–95, lifted by
Nicholas II's fiat. The world *première was abroad* (Théâtre Libre, Paris, 1888) while still
banned in Russia. A medium-genre encoding: the print/stage split is itself a channel
mechanism.

### 4. `1886-1890-the-fruits-of-enlightenment` — drama, stage-only ban (satirical inverse)

**Marquee.** The satirical INVERSE of Power of Darkness — comedy by and for Tolstoy's own
class (the family theatre, then the court), using the peasants as the MORAL MEASURE against
which the gentry's spiritualist idleness is judged. Twin-born with PoD in the same 1886
notebook (`fe-com-simultaneous`). Tolstoy named the irony himself: staging the satire of idle
gentry amusement was itself idle gentry amusement, «в усиленной мере» (`fe-biryukov-mocks`).

**Genre:** drama (comedy/satire). **Channel:** print *free and uncensored* in Russia 1891
(`fe-com-uncensored`); but Alexander III barred the *public stage* while permitting *amateur*
performance — «на любительских же театрах она может быть разрешена» (`fe-com-stageban`). A
play mocking the idle gentry literally confined to performance BY the gentry. The print/stage
channel split again, inverted.

### 5. `1887-1889-the-kreutzer-sonata` — ban lifted by Sofia's personal audience with the Tsar

**Marquee.** How far Tolstoy endorses Pozdnyshev: the answer is *proposition owned in propria
persona* (the Afterword, in his own voice) but *modality withheld* — «Целомудрие не есть
правило или предписание, а идеал» (`pos-chastity-ideal-not-rule`). For the theme: the novella
was print-*banned* (Dec 1889) and swept Russia *underground* in lithographed/hectographed
copies (the 8th redaction — a forerunner of samizdat, `comm-8th-lithographed`), printed abroad
1890 ahead of Russia, and reached legal Russian print *only* via Sofia Tolstaya's personal
audience with Alexander III (Apr 1891) — and then only inside the Collected Works vol. 13,
carrying ~200 distortions.

**Genre:** fiction (novella) + its own-voice Afterword (treatise-pole companion). **Channel:**
banned → underground samizdat → abroad-first → legal-Russia only by personal imperial
dispensation, Collected-Works-only. The channel arc that the personal-audience workaround
uniquely illustrates.

### 6. `1897-1898-what-is-art` — the theory of art-for-the-people + the two-stage censorship

**Marquee.** Art relocated from beauty to the *transmission of feeling* (заражение /
infection); good art = the brotherhood-of-all religious consciousness; the famous
self-condemnation of his own novels. For the theme it is doubly load-bearing: (a) it
*theorises* the народный рассказ — the universal-art criterion the folk tales practise; (b)
its own publication is a censorship exhibit. Tolstoy declares the *English* edition the first
in its «настоящем виде» (`wia-pref-trueform`) because every Russian edition was mangled — first
Grot's lexical softening (всегда→иногда, церковное→католическое), then the spiritual censor
cutting passages and *inserting the redemption dogma* Tolstoy rejected, putting an alien
doctrine in his mouth (`wia-pref-censor-redemption`).

**Genre:** treatise (the DIRECT pole) — but a treatise that is itself censored, and that
contains the theory of the encoded genre. The narodnyi-rasskaz footnote-theory link.
**Channel:** censored Russian journal serial (Вопросы философии 1897–98) ↔ complete
*uncensored* text only in Maude's English abroad (1898). The direct treatise still could not
appear whole in legal Russian print.

### 7. `1896-1904-hadji-murat` — posthumous; the 1912 censor cuts

**Marquee.** Betrayal or fulfilment of What Is Art?: Tolstoy wrote his most sensuous late
fiction *in secret* (`c-secret-composition`) while publicly teaching art must be moral, and
called writing it «баловство», «глупого», «совестно». The mirrored critique of Nicholas I and
Shamil (two poles of absolutism). He decided NOT to publish in his lifetime
(`c-not-publish-lifetime`) — so the censorship story is *posthumous*.

**Genre:** fiction (novella). **Channel = withheld-then-posthumous-split-publication:** the
1912 censored Moscow text (Belgard cut the Nicholas-I chapter as «недопустимым… нападкам» on
the Tsar's memory, `c-belgard-verdict`) vs Chertkov's *uncensored Berlin* (Ladyzhnikov)
edition restoring all cuts (`c-berlin-uncensored`). State/dynasty censorship of a *dead*
author — the secret-fiction + abroad-restoration channel.

### 8. `1890-1898-father-sergius` — posthumous fiction (cut Nicholas-I strand)

**Marquee.** The pursuit of holiness/asceticism is itself a form of pride — слава людская, the
craving for human esteem — and humble anonymous service (Pashenka) is the real thing. Tolstoy
stated it himself to Chertkov: «главная борьба… с славой людской» (`E11`). Finished 1898 *for
the Doukhobor emigration* (`E13`) — the censored-content material funding a persecuted sect's
escape.

**Genre:** fiction (novella). **Channel:** withheld in life → posthumous 1911. The Moscow 1911
first edition *cut* the Nicholas-I references (the cadet-corps adoration; the fiancée-as-
Tsar's-mistress strand), restored in brackets only in the Berlin 1912 edition (`bans` /
`censoredVersionNotes`, evidence E6). Same posthumous + abroad-restoration pattern as Hadji
Murat.

### 9. `1889-1909-the-devil` — posthumous fiction (author-suppressed, secret)

**Marquee.** The "devil" of the title is not the peasant Stepanida but Evgeny's own divided,
involuntary will — the title is the protagonist's self-deceiving projection, staged *as*
projection (sharpest in the murder-ending variant: «Да нет никакого Бога. Есть дьявол. И это
она», `E9`). Drafted inside the Kreutzer fortnight (Nov 1889), titled and given a second
ending c.1909.

**Genre:** fiction (novella). **Channel = author-suppression / secrecy:** named «секрет» at
conception (`E21`); *withdrawn* from the Doukhobor-fund sale in 1898 because «мотив один и тот
же, что в Отце Сергие» (`E22`); kept hidden from Sofia for twenty years (discovered 1909,
marital crisis); first published *posthumously* 1911. No state censorship — the suppression is
entirely the author's own. The withheld/secret pole in its purest form.

### 10. `1894-1895-master-and-man` — COMPARATOR: legal Russian publication, uncensored

**Marquee.** On Life given narrative flesh — salvation routed through the exploiter (Brekhunov
covers his freezing workman with his own body; «он — Никита, а Никита — он», `E1`). Keystone
compositional fact: the substitutionary self-dissolution was a *late, proof-stage* addition
(вариант № 33, рук. 11), absent from the first draft where Nikita survives alone (`E10`/`E12`)
— argument distilling into the story's doctrinal heart only at the last.

**Genre:** fiction (novella). **Channel = THE COMPARATOR (clean legal Russian print):**
published March 1895 in Severny Vestnik + Posrednik simultaneously, *not censored*, 15,000
copies in four days. The control case that shows the channel poles above are *choices forced by
content*, not Tolstoy's default — when the content was sayable, he published it legally,
cheaply, at home. (No encryption needed; included to mark the baseline. Carries a Thread-2
compression datapoint in the late-addition of the doctrinal core.)

### 11. `fire-metaphor` — COMPRESSION ENDPOINT (the anthologies + the distilled aphorism)

**Marquee (for this theme).** The fire/light metaphor as Tolstoy's single most compressed
vehicle: the same Luke 12:49 spark («Огонь принес я на землю…») distilling across thirty years
from the Gospel harmony (1880) → the late treatises → the *anthologies* → the final aphorism.
The dive directly touches На каждый день and Путь жизни — the Thread-2 endpoints — and shows
the *mechanism* of compression: the full Gospel harmony keeps «и как желаю» and amplifies the
spark of God-consciousness, while the *condensed* Gospel in Brief / French Abrégé strip the
first-person longing and collapse the metaphor to a bare simile (`brief-luke12-49` /
`abrege-luke12-49`). Compression visibly *loses* the inner longing — the cost of distillation,
documented.

**Genre:** spans gospel-harmony / treatise / letters / diaries / fiction / Круг чтения.
**Channel:** mostly legal (the metaphor is doctrinally safe) — relevant to Thread 2, not
Thread 1. Endpoints: Путь жизни (Tom 45, 1910 — his last book; `put-luke12-49-slow`,
`put-candle-without-fire`, `put-divine-fire`); Круг чтения (Tom 41, 1906; `krug-chtenija-sparks`);
On Life (Tom 26, 1887; `ozhizni-grass-fire`). The diary root of the "scattered fires"
compression: «Бог раскидал огонь в дрова» (1883, `diary-1883-fire-in-logs`).

## PART 3 — Cross-dive synthesis notes (for the index.md truth × form × channel map)

**The channel spectrum, ordered by concealment (most direct → most hidden):**
1. **Legal Russian print, uncensored** — `master-and-man` (1895). The baseline: when content
   was sayable, Tolstoy published it cheaply at home. No encryption.
2. **Legal Russian print, censored** — `what-is-art` journal serial; the народные рассказы of
   `stories-for-the-people` (arrests + the spiritual censor); the Posrednik tales of
   `1903-folk-tales` (two of five).
3. **Print free / stage banned** (genre as channel) — the two dramas: `power-of-darkness`
   (1887–95 stage ban) and `fruits-of-enlightenment` (stage-only ban, amateur allowed).
4. **Abroad-first / abroad-only** — `kreutzer-sonata` (foreign 1890 ahead of Russia);
   `what-is-art` (complete text only in Maude's English); `1903-folk-tales`'s «Разрушение ада»
   («Свободное слово», Russia 1917 only).
5. **Underground / samizdat** — `kreutzer-sonata` (8th redaction lithographed/hectographed).
6. **Legal Russia by personal dispensation only** — `kreutzer-sonata` (Sofia's audience with
   the Tsar → Collected Works only).
7. **Posthumous, censored in Russia + restored abroad** — `hadji-murat` (Moscow cut / Berlin
   full); `father-sergius` (Moscow 1911 cut / Berlin 1912 restored).
8. **Withheld / secret (author-suppressed)** — `hadji-murat` (decided not to publish in life);
   `the-devil` (secret 20 years, withdrawn from sale).

**The compression arc (Thread 2), ordered chronologically toward the endpoint:**
- On Life (1887, `fire-metaphor:ozhizni-grass-fire`) — the metaphysics in a single image.
- The 1903 «это ты» parables (`1903-folk-tales:E15/E16/E17`) — argument → story → the bald
  tat-tvam-asi proposition, all three registers in one cluster.
- Круг чтения (1906, `fire-metaphor:krug-chtenija-sparks`) — the anthology as aphorism-store.
- Путь жизни (1910, `fire-metaphor:put-*`) — the last book; the metaphor as final compressed
  aphorism, posed as a question.
- The *cost* of compression: `fire-metaphor:brief-luke12-49` (the condensed Gospel strips the
  first-person longing the full harmony kept) — distillation loses something, documented.

**Where the two threads fuse:** `1903-folk-tales` (the народный рассказ is at once the
encrypted-forbidden-truth form AND the most compressed statement of the unity-of-life doctrine)
and `what-is-art` (the censored treatise that *theorises* the encoded universal-art form). These
two dives are the theme's hinge: encryption (Thread 1) and compression (Thread 2) meet in the
parable.

**Gaps this map does NOT cover (for the fresh-sweep queue, not this synthesis):** the explicit
«не могу сказать» / «нельзя печатать» hunt in the un-mined letters (Toms 73–82) and Chertkov
correspondence (Toms 88–89); the structural read of the four anthologies as such (only
`fire-metaphor`'s datapoints are reused here, not a structural anatomy of Мысли мудрых людей /
На каждый день); the un-dived direct-treatise comparators (Tom 34–35).

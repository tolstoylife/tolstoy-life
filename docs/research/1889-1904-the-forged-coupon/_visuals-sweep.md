## Channel 3 — Manuscript facsimiles

Primary autograph materials are held at two institutions. No publicly accessible digital facsimiles were found. All records are metadata-only provenance entries.

### Archival holding summary (from PSS Vol. 36 textological apparatus, confirmed via tolstoy-lit.ru)

The complete manuscript record for «Фальшивый купон» survives in six distinct manuscript groupings:

1. **3-page autograph** (ИРЛИ, shifr 22.5.16) — opening of Ch. 1 only; written on both sides of 2 quarto sheets plus beginning of a 3rd; dated March 3–4 (year not specified in sources, c. 1902).
2. **10-page draft** (ГТМ, АЧ folder 78) — first 5 pages = copy of Ch. 1 autograph in Chertkov's hand, corrected by Tolstoy; continuation in Tolstoy's autograph; 3 quarter-sheets with Ch. XI and XIII text (typed and corrected by Tolstoy).
3. **151-page fair copy** (ИРЛИ, 22.5.16) — all autograph and copyist material for the full novella; pages numbered by the ИРЛИ librarian.
4. **140-page typed/handwritten copy** (ИРЛИ, 22.5.16) — typed by M. L. Obolenskaya, A. L. Tolstaya, and Yu. I. Igumnova; Tolstoy's corrections in most chapters.
5. **ГТМ АЧ folder 82** — additional draft sheets.

## Channel 4 — Adaptations / afterlife

### A4-01 — Bresson, *L'Argent* (1983) — PRIMARY ADAPTATION
- **id:** A4-01
- **type:** film-still / poster (metadata only — rights-reserved)
- **subject:** *L'Argent* (1983), directed and written by Robert Bresson
- **adaptation basis:** Part I of «Фальшивый купон» only (the coupon-as-prop schoolboy prank and its chain of consequences); Bresson began the script in 1977
- **festival:** Cannes Film Festival 1983 — Prix de la mise en scène (Best Director), shared with Andrei Tarkovsky for *Nostalghia*
- **runtime:** 83 minutes
- **release:** France, 18 May 1983; Bresson's final film
- **home video:** Criterion Collection, spine #886 (DVD + Blu-ray)
- **poster/stills access:** Rights-reserved — held by the Bresson estate and Criterion Collection; no free-use stills identified
- **rights:** rights-reserved
- **licence:** rights-reserved
- **usable:** false
- **localPath:** —
- **source URLs:**
  - Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%27Argent_(1983_film)
  - Criterion: https://www.criterion.com/films/27588-l-argent
- **note:** The adaptation is confined to Part I of the novella. Bresson does not follow Part II (the chain of redemption). ## How to obtain manuscript facsimiles

- **ИРЛИ (Pushkin House):** https://pushkinskijdom.ru/ → «Читальный зал» → remote reproduction form. Cite shifr 22.5.16.
- **ГТМ (State Tolstoy Museum):** https://tolstoymuseum.ru/ → «Научный отдел» / архив. Cite АЧ, папки 78 и 82. The KAMIS online catalogue (https://tolstoy-iss.kamiscloud.ru/) may show low-res preview thumbnails for manuscript items — search «фальшивый купон» in the Рукописи collection.
- **Goskatalog:** https://goskatalog.ru/portal/#/collections?q=Фальшивый+купон+Толстой — may return digitised thumbnails for registered museum objects.

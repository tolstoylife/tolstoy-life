# Run-report — `1882-1886-what-then-must-we-do`

Work-dive on Tolstoy's *What Then Must We Do?* (Так что же нам делать?), the fourth Prophet-period
panel and the economic/social application of the *What I Believe* ethic. Run 2026-06-06, in-session
unattended (the A-Confession-pilot model), medium visual intensity. Followed the handoff from the
*What I Believe* session.

## What was covered

- **Deep read** of all 40 chapters + the «О переписи в Москве» appeal; keystone passages extracted
  byte-faithful (the failed-charity narrative & «I sit on a man's back»; money-as-slavery + the Fiji
  parable; the science / division-of-labour critique = «захват чужого труда»; the three answers &
  bread-labour / Bondarev; the contested final chapter on women).
- **Genesis triply grounded:** the Jan-1882 census + «О переписи»; the 1 Jan 1883 property/violence
  diary entry and the 1884 estate-reform plan; the Dec-1884 Alekseev bridge and the 1885–86 Bondarev
  letters. Composition chronology 1882→1886 reconstructed from the apparatus (begun early 1882, **not**
  1884; stop-start; intensive Dec 1884; finishing through 1885–86).
- **Publication/suppression** (distinct from *What I Believe*'s clean ban): serialization refused at
  *Русская мысль* (Jan 1885); cut fragments in *Русское богатство* (1885); cut full text in the 5th-ed.
  vol. 12 (10 Apr 1886) after Pobedonostsev's Synod refusal + Ivantsov-Platonov's notes; fuller text
  abroad (Geneva/Elpidine «Какова моя жизнь?» 1886); complete text 1902 (Chertkov, Christchurch).
  English: Hapgood 1887 → Wiener 1904 → Maude 1925.
- **The family conflict over the book** (apparatus): S. A. Tolstaya objected to the named-family
  episodes, not the argument; Tolstoy conceded the personal forms and held the thought, while she
  prepared the edition and petitioned the censor — complicating the Sofia-centred frame.
- **Scholarship triangulation** (7 entries) + the newest reading (Nicolosi 2024, slum-literature).
- **Visuals:** 8 PD images cached (keystone = Repin's 1887 *Tolstoy Ploughing*).

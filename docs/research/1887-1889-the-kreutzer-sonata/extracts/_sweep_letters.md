# Letters sweep — The Kreutzer Sonata

Sweep of Tom 64 (PSS, letters ~1887–1889) and Tom 65 (letters ~1890–1891) for correspondence bearing on the novella, the Afterword, publication, censorship, lithograph circulation, and key people.

**Method:** `rg` across all 562 v64/v65 letter files for `Крейцер|повесть|послесловие|цензур|запрет|литограф|гектограф`; 116 candidates matched; all extracted via `extract_tei.py --choice=reg --notes=auto`; 53 letters explicitly name "Крейцер"; remaining candidates filtered by context (most are unrelated — Власть тьмы, О жизни, or generic censorship references). 39 letters included below as materially relevant.

**Coverage gap:** No letter from Sofia Andreevna (S.A. Tolstaya) to Tolstoy is present in this TEI corpus; her letters are not in `primary-sources/tolstoydigital-TEI/texts/letters/`. Her audience with the Tsar (May 1891) is recorded only as Tolstoy reporting it to a third party (see v65_310 below). No letter from Chertkov to Tolstoy is in this corpus either.

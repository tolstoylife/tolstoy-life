## Reading

- **Title:** *Leo Tolstoj — Hans lif och hans verk* ("Leo Tolstoy — His life and his work").
- **Subtitle:** *Själfbiografiska bref och anteckningar samt biografiska uppgifter* ("Autobiographical letters and notes, together with biographical data"). Pre-1906 Swedish spelling reform orthography (*själfbiografiska*, *bref*, *anteckningar*).
- **Compiler:** Paul Birukoff (German transliteration of Бирюков; this is the spelling Birukoff himself used in Western European correspondence, including his work with the Diederichs German edition).
- **Authorisation:** *"samt granskade af Leo Tolstoj"* — "and reviewed by Leo Tolstoy." Direct claim of authorial review on the title page. This is the strongest possible advertising of Tolstoy involvement; whether it reflects review of the Russian source or of the Swedish translation itself is a §3 question.
- **Translation status:** *"Bemyndigad öfversättning från ryska originalet"* — "Authorised translation from the Russian original." Translator Erik Nordenström.
- **Volume designation:** *Andra delen* — "Second part." Per the handoff, Johan owns Första delen 1, Första delen 2, and Andra delen (this volume) — three physical books covering Russian Vols 1–2.
- **Publisher:** P. A. Norstedt & Söners förlag, Stockholm. Norstedt is one of Sweden's oldest and most established publishing houses (founded 1823; Royal Court printer). The publisher signals editorial weight, not a small or unauthorised venture.
- **Publisher monogram:** intertwined cursive letters reading approximately "PAN&L" or "TAN&L" — Norstedt's house mark of the period; exact resolution unread.
- **Stamped mark:** small purple/violet rubber stamp at the foot of the page, partially legible — likely a previous owner's mark, an antiquarian's stock mark, or a library stamp. Not yet identifiable from the photograph.

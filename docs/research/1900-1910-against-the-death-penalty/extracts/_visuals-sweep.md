# Visuals sweep — "Against the death penalty" / Tolstoy 1900–1910

Sweep date: 2026-06-13
Channel: Wikimedia Commons (single-channel, light-to-medium intensity)
Images downloaded: 4 (all verified non-empty: 3 JPEG, 1 PNG)
Work-orders (not openly available on Commons in this sweep): 6

This sweep covers the anti-capital-punishment cluster of 1900–1910: «Не убий» (after the
assassination of Umberto I, 1900), «Не убий никого» (1907), «Смертная казнь и христианство»
(1908, against A. A. Stolypin's defence of the gallows), «Не могу молчать» (1908; visuals for
that essay live in the sibling dive — cross-referenced below, not refetched).

## Work-orders — not openly available on Commons in this sweep

1. **The 1905–1909 Russian execution wave / "Stolypin's necktie"** — a PD photograph or political
   cartoon of executions / gallows during the post-1905 repression. SENSITIVE: any such image
   depicts real killings; caption must note this and avoid sensationalism. Searched Commons by
   keyword (English and Russian — «казнь», «повешение», "hanging", "Riga 1906", "gallows") and by
   category (Category:Capital_punishment_in_the_Russian_Empire, Category:Executions_in_the_Russian_Empire)
   — both categories returned empty via the API and keyword search returned only PDF books, no usable
   photograph or drawing. WORK-ORDER: try a dedicated archive (Library of Congress, GARF, or the
   1905-revolution illustrated press / Sytin lubok cartoons) outside Commons. This is the one
   thematic gap in the sweep.

2. **A. A. Stolypin (Alexander Arkadievich Stolypin)** — the Novoe Vremya journalist who defended
   the gallows and is the direct interlocutor of «Смертная казнь и христианство» (1908). No portrait
   found: Commons "Alexander Stolypin" / "Alexandr Stolypin" results resolve to the PM Pyotr Stolypin
   (File:Alexandr Stolypin.jpg, 1892, is actually Pyotr) or to unrelated people (A. N. Naumov).
   WORK-ORDER: likely needs a Russian-language source (Novoe Vremya staff photos, RGALI) — may have
   no easily accessible PD image. The cross-referenced PM Pyotr Stolypin portrait can stand in for
   the Stolypin name with an explicit caption that it is the brother, not A. A.

3. **N. E. Felten** (publisher of «Обновление»; arrested 1907 for printing «Не убий») — no PD image
   expected and none found. WORK-ORDER.

4. **Title page / facsimile — «Листки Свободного слова»** (Chertkov's émigré series, England) —
   not on Commons. WORK-ORDER: try archive.org or the Tolstoy museum digital holdings.

5. **Title page / facsimile — the «Обновление» 1906 edition** — not on Commons. WORK-ORDER:
   archive.org / RGB (Russian State Library) digital.

6. **Title page / facsimile — I. P. Ladyzhnikov (Berlin) «Смертная казнь и христианство», 1909** —
   not on Commons. WORK-ORDER: the émigré-archive pair vtoraya-literatura.com / imwerden.de is the
   canonical first stop for pre-1940 Russian-language émigré scans (see project memory), then
   archive.org.

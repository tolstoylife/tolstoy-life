**Hit count: 0 materially-relevant diary entries.**

As the brief anticipated, Tolstoy's diary in this period is almost dormant — he kept very
few entries between 1881 and 1887. The corpus holds exactly **five 1886 entries** and **one
early-1887 entry**, none of which mentions the play, its genesis, its staging, or its
censorship. The play-period witness is carried entirely by the **letters** (see
`_sweep_letters.md`, 10 hits).

The two entries closest in date to the start of composition are 18 October 1886 (the play
was begun ~late October) and 3 February 1887 (mid-staging/censorship). Both are purely
metaphysical. The relevant October entry, in full:

> **18 Октября 86.** Как странно и невероятно кажется сначала, чтобы будущая жизнь моя была
> только во всей деятельности, произведенной моей жизнью и не только в душах людей, но и в
> мире вообще. Так же странно и невероятно показалось бы зародышу, что будущая жизнь
> зародыша будет та сложная деятельность моего тела. А аналогия полная.

*(working English)* "18 October 86. How strange and improbable it seems at first that my
future life should consist only in all the activity produced by my life — and not only in
the souls of people but in the world in general. Just as strange and improbable would it
seem to an embryo that the embryo's future life is the complex activity of my body. And the
analogy is complete."

*Significance:* none for the play directly, but it is worth noting for the dive that the
metaphysics Tolstoy was privately turning over in the very weeks he wrote the play — life
continuing as the moral *activity* it produces in the world — is the same key as the
self-watching he reports in the letters (e.g. the 14–15 Dec 1886 Ge letter on praise and
vanity, `v63_586_l`). The diary gives the inner weather, not the work-record.
